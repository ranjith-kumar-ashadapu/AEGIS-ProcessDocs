# Interpreting Risk Prediction Scores

## Executive Summary
Project AEGIS does not just monitor active disasters; its core value lies in predicting them. The CARIVIX AI engine analyzes millions of data points across weather patterns, social media sentiment and news reports to generate Risk Prediction Scores. This document explains how to interpret these scores to make proactive deployment decisions.

## AI Confidence Levels
Throughout the GIS Intelligence Dashboard and the main Home screen, you will notice floating intelligence cards displaying an **AI Confidence** percentage. 
- This percentage represents the AI model's certainty that a specific predicted event will occur within the designated timeframe.
- **High Confidence (>85%):** Highly probable event. Immediate standby mobilization is recommended.
- **Moderate Confidence (50% - 84%):** Developing pattern. Continue monitoring via the `Violet (Prediction)` threat indicators on the GIS map.
- **Low Confidence (<50%):** Anomalous data detected, but insufficient evidence to trigger a formal warning.

## Threat Level Thresholds
Risk prediction scores are directly tied to the color-coded threat indicators on your dashboard:
- **Prediction (Violet - `#3882F6` / `#38BDF8`):** The earliest stage of a threat. The AI has detected a pattern and a risk score is generating. 
- **Warning (Amber - `#F59E0B` / `#D97706`):** The risk score has crossed the critical threshold, indicating high probability.
- **Critical (Red - `#EF4444` / `#DC2626`):** The prediction has materialized into an active crisis.

## Strategic Application
When reviewing a risk score, always cross-reference it with the **Data Sources** metric. A high prediction score backed by 50+ diverse data sources (e.g., satellite imagery combined with on-the-ground social media panic) is highly actionable, whereas a prediction relying on a single data source may require manual verification.
