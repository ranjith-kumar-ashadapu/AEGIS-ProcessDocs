# Getting Started

## Executive Summary
Welcome to Project AEGIS, India's AI-powered crisis intelligence platform. This guide covers the initial steps for logging in, understanding the core interface elements and setting up your workspace for crisis monitoring. 

## System Access and Authentication
Project AEGIS is a restricted-access platform designed for government and institutional authorities. 

1. **Accessing the Portal:** Navigate to the secure AEGIS URL provided by your system administrator.
2. **Authentication:** Enter your secure credentials.
3. **Profile Verification:** Upon logging in, verify your access level by clicking on your User Profile at the bottom of the left sidebar. The profile popup will display your Role, Department, Organization, Email and Access Level.

## Navigating the Platform
The platform uses a collapsible left-side navigation bar for quick access to all major modules:
- **Home:** Platform overview and high-level statistics.
- **Dashboard:** The main center for active alerts, threat distribution and AI predictions.
- **GIS Intelligence:** The spatial mapping tool for visualizing geographic hotspots.
- **Response Teams:** Tools for coordinating and deploying active and standby missions.
- **Reports:** Analytics and downloadable AI-generated threat assessments.

## Theme Customization & Typography
To ensure optimal visibility in various operating environments, AEGIS supports dual themes. The entire platform utilizes the **Montserrat** font family Bold for headings, SemiBold for section titles and Regular for body content to maintain a modern, readable enterprise appearance.
