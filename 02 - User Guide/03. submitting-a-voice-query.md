# Submitting a Voice Query

## Executive Summary
The AEGIS AI Assistant is a core feature that allows users to interact with the system using natural language. Instead of manually filtering through datasets, users can submit voice or text queries to instantly generate insights, maps and recommended actions.

## Accessing the AI Assistant
1. Locate the **AI Assistant** button, typically found in the top navigation bar or via a floating action button on the dashboard.
2. Clicking this will trigger the assistant modal. The modal is designed as a centered overlay with a glassmorphism background, allowing you to maintain visibility of the dashboard behind it while focusing on your query.

## How to Formulate a Query
For the best results, structure your queries to specify the **event**, **location** or **metric** you are looking for. 

**Examples of effective queries:**
- *"Which regions are showing increasing flood risk and misinformation spread?"*
- *"Show regions with increasing crisis risk."*

## Processing and Output
Once your query is submitted, the AI engine will process the request across weather datasets, news sources, social media and government reports. 

The system will respond by automatically generating:
- **AI-Generated Insights:** A text and voice summary of the situation.
- **Risk Prediction Scores:** Quantifiable threat levels.
- **GIS Hotspot Map:** Automatically adjusting the background map to highlight the queried region.
- **Recommended Actions:** Strategic steps for response coordination.
