# Reading GIS Maps and Hotspots

## Executive Summary
The GIS Intelligence Dashboard is the visual core of Project AEGIS. It provides real-time spatial intelligence, allowing users to track active threats, safe zones and predicted crisis areas on an interactive map of India.

## Interface Controls
* **Search:** Use the top search bar to immediately jump to a specific state, city or coordinates.
* **Filters:** Click the `Filters` button to toggle specific smart layers, such as population density, weather patterns or resource shortages.
* **Zoom Controls:** Use the `+` and `-` icons to zoom in on specific districts or zoom out for a national view.

## Understanding Threat Levels using Color Codes
The map utilizes a strict, color-coded threat indicator system to instantly communicate the severity of a hotspot. Familiarize yourself with these indicators:

- **Critical Risk:** Immediate, active crisis requiring urgent attention. Indicates using Heat Red in Dark mode and Threat Red in Light mode.
- **Warning:** Developing threat with a high probability of escalating. Indicates using Amber in Dark mode and Amber Gold in Light mode.
- **Prediction:** AI-forecasted danger zone based on pattern analysis. Indicates using the Primary/Secondary Accents.
- **Safe Zone:** Monitored regions currently clear of active threats. Indicates using Emerald in Dark mode and Secure Green in Light mode.

## Interacting with Hotspots
Clicking on any colored hotspot marker will pull up region-specific analytics. This includes the localized threat distribution, the exact AI confidence level regarding the prediction and any active response teams currently deployed to those coordinates.
