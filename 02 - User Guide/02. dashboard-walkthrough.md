# Dashboard Walkthrough

## Executive Summary
The Crisis Intelligence Dashboard is the primary operational center of the Project AEGIS platform. Designed with a clean analytics layout, this dashboard allows crisis management teams to monitor active threats and AI-generated predictions at a glance.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The top section of the dashboard features four primary metric cards that provide an immediate snapshot of the national crisis status. These utilize the official AEGIS design system colors to instantly communicate status:
- **Active Threats (Alert/Risk Red):** Displayed in Heat Red (`#EF4444`) for Dark Theme and Threat Red (`#DC2626`) for Light Theme. Represents ongoing critical situations requiring immediate attention.
- **AI Predictions (Primary Accent):** Forecasts generated by the CARIVIX AI engine, highlighting developing risks before they escalate.
- **Safe Zones (Success Green):** Displayed in Emerald (`#10B981`) for Dark Theme and Secure Green (`#059669`) for Light Theme. Represents monitored regions currently clear of immediate danger.
- **Data Sources:** The total number of active, verified data streams currently feeding the intelligence system.

## Alerts and Threat Analysis
The central section of the dashboard categorizes and visualizes incoming crisis data:
- **Recent Critical Alerts:** A live, prioritized feed of the most severe warnings. 
- **Threat Distribution:** A visual bar chart that breaks down current risks by category.

## Operational Tracking
The lower section integrates deployment tracking to ensure seamless coordination:
- **Active Response Operations:** A quick-view summary of deployed response teams, their current assignment and operational status.
- **Quick GIS Overview:** A condensed, interactive map widget for glancing at spatial data without navigating to the full GIS module.
