# Project AEGIS Overview

**Project Theme:** Project AEGIS
**Core Identity:** AI-Powered National Crisis Intelligence & Prediction System
**Official Tagline:** "Project AEGIS - Where AI Predicts, Maps and Protects."

## Executive Summary
Project AEGIS represents a paradigm shift from traditional dashboard projects. CARIVIX is developing a futuristic, comprehensive intelligence system designed to proactively manage crises. Instead of simply reporting past data, the system is engineered to predict, map and protect.

## Core Capabilities
- **Disaster Monitoring:** Actively tracks and monitors natural and man-made disasters.
- **Misinformation Detection:** Identifies and tracks the spread of false information during critical events.
- **Data Analysis:** Analyzes large-scale social, media and public datasets to extract actionable insights.
- **Predictive Modeling:** Forecasts and predicts potential risk zones before they escalate.
- **Voice-Driven Intelligence:** Generates automated, voice-enabled intelligence reports for rapid briefing.
- **Live Visualization:** Maps out situations dynamically on live geo-maps for real-time situational awareness.
